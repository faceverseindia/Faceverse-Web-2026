FaceVerse

25–27 Sep2026
New DelhiAIIMS & The Ashok
2 halls50+ faculty

A universe of the face — mapped by the people who reshape it.

Look closely at the mark: a mesh wraps a half-face, and small red crosses fall on the points that decide a result — the radix, the tip-defining points, the malar high point, the mandibular border. Every surgeon in the room reads those landmarks differently, and that difference is the whole conversation.

FaceVerse India was built around that conversation. Not one philosophy, but the full spread of them under one roof — preservation and structure, scalpel and syringe, the reconstructive ward and the aesthetic suite — placed close enough together to argue productively.

Two days. Two halls. Every subspecialty that touches the face.

Day 0 opens at AIIMS with hands-on workshops. The summit then moves to The Ashok, where rhinoplasty and deep-plane facelift run alongside regenerative aesthetics, facial palsy, reconstruction and gender-affirming surgery — with free papers, panels and a quiz threaded through. This guide exists so none of that is hard to navigate.
