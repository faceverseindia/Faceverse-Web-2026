The People Behind It

The Founders

FaceVerse India is founded by two facial plastic & ENT surgeons who believe India's finest deserve a stage of their own.

Honorary Chairman · FaceVerse India

Dr Rakesh Kumar

Father of Facial Plastic Surgery in India
HOD, Department of Otorhinolaryngology & Head–Neck Surgery, AIIMS New Delhi

A towering figure in Indian ENT and head–neck surgery whose life’s work laid the foundation for facial plastic surgery in the country. FaceVerse convenes under his honorary chairmanship.

Dr Shreya Garg

Co-founder · FaceVerse India
Facial Plastic & ENT Surgeon

Partner at Aloysia Eveite, the facial plastic & aesthetic surgery practice in Safdarjung Enclave, South Delhi. A surgeon whose work spans rhinoplasty and facelift, and one half of the voice behind FaceVerse's demonstration-first philosophy.

Dr Prateek Sharma

Co-founder · FaceVerse India
Facial Plastic & ENT Surgeon · MBBS (MAMC, New Delhi) · MS ENT (AIIMS New Delhi) · Commonwealth Fellow (UK)

Known as “the facelift surgeon,” pioneering the deep plane facelift in India. Partner at Aloysia Eveite and founder of Eveite, with a practice devoted entirely to the face — anatomy inside, aesthetics outside.

Learn from them in person.
